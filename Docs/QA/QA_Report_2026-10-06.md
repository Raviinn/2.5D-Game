## Bugs and problems (most important first)

### 1. You can walk off the edge of the world and fall forever — HIGH
- **What happens:** walk east, west or south past the trees to the edge of the 100 × 100 m ground. The player walks off it and falls into a sky-blue void, with no death, no respawn and no way back except loading a save.
  - After 6 seconds the player was 270 m below the ground and still falling.
  - Something blocks the north edge.
- **Evidence:** `screenshots/bug1_fell_off_world.png`
- **Suggested fix:** invisible walls around the playable area, plus a safety net that puts the player back on solid ground if they ever fall below the world.

### 2. Your Unity project is missing four setup steps — HIGH (setup, not code)
Your Unity Editor log from your last session (5 Oct) shows setups 1–11 and 14 have been run, but not these:

| Setup | What's missing until you run it |
|---|---|
| **12** Sprite Shadows | Characters and trees don't darken in shade |
| **13** Smarter Enemies | The third bandit (Bandit_C) |
| **15** Night Danger & Fatigue | Fatigue on the player (no Tired / Exhausted) |
| **16** Main Menu & Save Slots | The title screen (the game boots straight into the world) |

**Fix:** run them in order: **Beast → Setup → Milestone 12, 13, 15, 16**. All are safe to re-run. In the test copy they all ran cleanly, and running 16 twice was safe too.

### 3. Holding F on a harvested tile replants and waters it straight away — MEDIUM (please confirm)
Holding F to harvest a row works the tile through its cycle: harvest → plant (uses a seed) → water, all within about a second. That's handy for working a row, but it spends seeds when you only meant to harvest.

**Option:** stop a held F after a harvest until you release the key.

### 4. Fading notifications leave an empty dark box — LOW
When a notice on the left of the HUD (+3 Turnip, +25 gold…) fades out, its text fades but the dark box behind it stays fully visible for its last second. The same applies to every fading "pill", such as the interaction prompt and the climbing hints.

- **Evidence:** the empty box above "+3 Turnip" in `screenshots/bug4_empty_notification_box.png`
- **Cause:** `UITheme.Pill` sets its own transparency and ignores the fade.
- **Fix:** multiply by the current fade (a one-line change).

### 5. Character screen cuts off skill requirements — LOW
Long skill header lines are cut off mid-sentence, for example "Whirlwind … Greatsword · Requires Combat" and "Shield Charge … Sword & Shield · Requires".

- **Evidence:** `screenshots/bug5_…png`
- **Fix:** wrap the line, or move the requirement to its own line.

### 6. Quest banners draw on top of open menus — LOW
"QUEST ACCEPTED" and other banners stay on screen over the inventory, character, journal and shop windows (same screenshot).

**Fix:** hold banners until the menu closes, or draw them behind menus.

### 7. Smaller polish items — LOW
- **Item details:** "Restores 30 health and 0 stamina". Hide the zero part.
- **Build Settings:** includes Unity's template scene `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity`, which would ship in builds. Remove it from the list.
- **Roots of the Blight:** if you already carry a Healroot when you first show Brenna the ash, she still says "Bring me a sprig" and you have to talk to her a second time.
- **Camera:** right next to a building, the house can fill half the screen. There's no fade-out of things between the camera and the player. See `screenshots/note_camera_blocked_by_house.png`.
- **Main menu:** buttons are ignored for the first frame or two after the menu appears. Players can't click that fast, but automation tripped on it.

## Design notes (working as built, worth a decision)
- **Death:** you respawn at the village start point after 2 s with full health and no penalty, not at your bed. This is the prototype rule from Milestone 2.
- **Dropped loot:** loot on the ground isn't saved; it vanishes on quit or load and despawns after 3 minutes.
- **Contracts:** the same three contracts every day, plus Town Patrol at Trusted (already in the known-limitations list).
